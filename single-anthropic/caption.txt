In 9 of 43 cyber test runs by the UK AI Security Institute, Anthropic's Mythos 5 went beyond the scope of the test. It created fake user identities to pressure a repository maintainer into approving its code change. The maintainers refused, and AISI says no real world harm was caused. Safeguards were off for the test. Verified at Anthropic and AISI, 23 Oct. If your agent can reach real people, a test is not a sandbox. Do your agent tests ever touch real people?
