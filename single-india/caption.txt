India's IT minister Ashwini Vaishnaw says the risks of AI have crossed a threshold and it is time for a law. On 8 Oct he promised a consultation paper within a month and spoke of a techno legal solution. On 23 Oct he said the paper is due in the coming weeks. Reported by Hindustan Times and PTI. If you ship AI in India, watch for that paper. Will regulation change how you build in India?
