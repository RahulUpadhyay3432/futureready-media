Privacy Policy

Kapyn Publisher · Effective 23 October 2026

What this app is

Kapyn Publisher is an internal publishing tool. The Kapyn team uses it only to publish our own posts (videos, images and captions) to our own Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts, and to read the status and basic insights of those posts. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

What data it uses

The app uses access tokens that Meta issues to the owner of our own accounts. With them it publishes our content and reads post status and insights for our own accounts. It does not collect, store or share personal data about other Facebook or Instagram users, and it does not use data for advertising or sell data to anyone. Tokens are kept privately by the operator and are never published.

Data deletion

The app keeps no personal data about anyone other than the account owner. If you believe it holds data about you, or you want any data removed, open a request at github.com/RahulUpadhyay3432/futureready-media/issues and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm when done.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
