OpenAI made its Decisions API generally available. It does not write: it estimates how likely something is true, picks one option, or gives a score. OpenAI says it is up to 10x faster than GPT 6 Luna through the Responses API and takes up to 400 questions in one request. Sierra reports under 280 ms at p95 and about 90% lower cost per call than its previous approach. Independent rankings still put it behind TypeSafe's Jev: TechCrunch reports the AIM System One Index and the Artificial Analysis Decision Index place Jev seventh and third, and OpenAI's model eighth and fourteenth. Sources: OpenAI, TechCrunch. Fast and cheap, or careful and right?
