An AI tutor that starts from what you already know.

LearnHarness (16 Oct 2026) teaches in four steps:
• Measure where your understanding ends
• Plan a map from there to your goal
• Teach one reasoning step at a time
• Practice with varied problems in separate sessions

It keeps a knowledge graph of what you learnt, so the next session does not start from scratch.

The catch: bring your own AI key. The app is free, but you pay your provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, OpenRouter and more) for usage, and a live meter shows the cost. Your data is stored on your machine, but model requests still go to your provider.

Free and open source (AGPL 3.0). 1,914 stars on GitHub on 26 Oct.
