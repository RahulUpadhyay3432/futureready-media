Ads on your TV's home screen? A repo from 17 Oct 2026 turns them off.

What cleanyourtv does:
• backs up your TV's app list and settings
• disables the maker's ad services, telemetry and content rows (nothing is uninstalled)
• installs a small launcher as your new home screen

Works on Android TV and Google TV. Fire TV gets the cleanup only (not the 2015 Fire TV Box). Samsung and LG run different systems.

The catch: you switch on USB debugging (the project says turn it off when you're done), and the phone app is made by a different developer. At your own risk: the project says changing a TV's software may affect its warranty or support. Changed your mind? The README promises "a one-line restore", since nothing is uninstalled.

Free and open source (MIT). 36,575 stars on GitHub on 26 Oct.
