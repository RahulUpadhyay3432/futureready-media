Meta's AI agent Muse just passed 10 million set-ups, about six weeks after it launched in the US and Canada. Zuckerberg says about 1 in 4 people who set it up use it on any given day, and TechCrunch reports that over a quarter of engaged shoppers averaged more than $200 a purchase. Agents are moving from answering questions to spending money. Sources: The Verge, TechCrunch, Fortune. Would you let an agent shop and buy for you?
