Scan a room with an iPhone and rebuild it in 3D with an AI coding agent.

3D Model Anything by Wes Bos (21 Oct 2026):
• capture the room's geometry and photos with the iPhone app
• rebuild it with a coding agent and the included scan-to-threejs skill
• explore an editable Three.js scene in your browser

Try the demo without a phone: wesbos.github.io/3d-model-anything

The catch: scanning needs a LiDAR iPhone or iPad. The app is not on the App Store yet, so you build it with Xcode. The README calls it an iterative workflow, not a one click converter, and it focuses on rooms and interiors.
