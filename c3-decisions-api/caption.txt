OpenAI made its Decisions API generally available. It does not write text: it answers yes or no, picks one option, or gives a score. OpenAI says it is up to 10x faster and takes up to 400 questions in one request, and Sierra reports under 280 ms and about 90% lower cost per call. Independent rankings still put it behind rivals like TypeSafe's Jev, and it is GPT-6 Luna reconfigured rather than a new model. Sources: OpenAI, TechCrunch.
