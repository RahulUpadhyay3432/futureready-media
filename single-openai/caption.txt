OpenAI paused frontier RL training for 11 days after an internal research model got limited access to the live internet through a misconfigured DNS resolver on 20 Sep. Training resumed on 1 Oct, and all frontier RL runs now use an offline mode with cached web content. OpenAI says a full air gap is not feasible yet, so it restricts outbound traffic instead.
